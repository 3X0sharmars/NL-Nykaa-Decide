EVIDENCE DECONSTRUCTION

What Helps Shoppers Decide?

Comparing available information at the point of decision. Decision hesitation is concentrated where seller specifications stop and customer evidence is absent.

WHAT THE SELLER SAYS Available
Product Identity & Claims Marketing-led titles and brand taglines present on 100% of examined catalog records.
Fabric Description Stated for 141 of 141 SKUs (100%), but without verified fibre percentages or fabric weights.
Fit Descriptor General silhouette stated for 138 of 141 SKUs (97.9%) — e.g. Regular, Slim, Loose, Relaxed.
Care Instructions Washing instructions present on majority of seller product cards.
WHAT BUYERS SAY Scarce (11.3%)
Customer Ratings Available on only 16 of 141 SKUs (11.3%). Average rating among rated items is 3.94★.
Written Review Observations 125 of 141 SKUs (88.7%) have 0 written reviews. Tail items receive almost no peer text.
Buyer Photos Zero buyer photos attached on examined tail items, preventing independent visual validation.
Long-Term Durability Shrinkage, wash fading, and fabric wear cannot be verified through buyer reviews.
WHAT WE DON'T KNOW Gap
Flat Garment Measurements Only 11 of 141 listings (7.8%) provide exact garment measurements in centimeters/inches.
Fabric Weight / GSM Stated on 0 of 141 listings (0.0%). Shoppers cannot quantify fabric thickness or seasonal warmth.
Opacity & Light Transmission Not stated on any listing. Buyers cannot determine see-through risk for light fabrics.
Stretch & Drape Recovery Technical stretch percentage missing on 90%+ of non-denim activewear and bottoms.

KEY RESEARCH FINDINGS

Empirical Research Signals

Verbatim customer signals and grounded observations from user interviews and catalog audits.

INTERVIEW SIGNAL · P02
“I trust the brand, but I’m still unsure about this particular product.”
SKU Confidence ≠ Brand Confidence. Even established brands leave shoppers uncertain when listing specifications and measurements are omitted.
CATALOG AUDIT SIGNAL
Listings describe material type, but omit garment measurements and weight entirely.
Fabric stated on 141 SKUs (100%), but garment measurements present on only 11 (7.8%) and fabric weight on 0 (0.0%).
PRIMARY WALK-THROUGH SIGNAL
Wishlists stall from decision uncertainty, not out-of-stock items.
22 of 22 walked-through wishlist items were in stock in the buyer’s size, and 21 of 22 were still actively wanted.
SYNTHESIS SIGNAL
Confidence is constrained by information gaps, not negative sentiment.
Low purchase confidence is caused by missing specifications and absent peer reviews rather than critical buyer complaints.
